# Visual review

All four first passes were reviewed for open combat space, future overlay readability, placement subtlety, and edge/center contrast.

## First-pass findings

- Shared top/bottom layout and usable bottom apron were clear.
- Ten identical solid wear patches looked too regular and too much like deliberately placed markers.
- Floor lighting was flat; side structures needed more material identity.
- No text, cards, units, or hard gameplay rectangles were present.

## Refinements applied to every version

- Replaced closed patches and side ovals with discontinuous, low-contrast worn strokes; varied the strokes deterministically.
- Added restrained floor shade and localized color bands near the corner lights.
- Preserved identical anchor positions, separator, and bottom overlay area.
- Kept material marks motivated by masonry joints, worn edges, cloth folds, wood grain, or botanical growth.

## Theme refinements

- Crimson Covenant: engraved brass corners, metal rim fasteners, embroidered crimson valance, folded fabric and restrained wood scuffs. Center stays dark and quiet.
- Azure Bastion: carved vault cornices, recessed pillar details, banner folds, subtle ledge wear, braziers with stronger material separation.
- Verdant Confluence: twisting bark, fern fans, clustered highlighted leaves, small glowing mushrooms, lily pads and a weathered divider. Plants stay at the edges.
- Hollow Sanctum: fractured buttresses, peripheral stone fragments, shallow incomplete floor engravings and worn brazier details. Central lanes remain unobstructed.

## Final visual check

Viewed all four refined backgrounds at 1920×1080. The unit hints are subordinate to the environment, the two sides read clearly, and the central and bottom overlay spaces remain quiet. Source uses a limited palette with discrete lighting bands; nearest-neighbor exports preserve the native pixel grid. All final images are opaque. No rendered text or gameplay entities are present.
